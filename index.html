More Than Just a Coffee Shop.

Founded in 2018, Rio Java was born from a simple dream: to create a space where the craft of coffee meets the heart of the Roseville community.

We don't just "serve" coffee; we celebrate it. Our beans are sourced from sustainable farms and roasted in small batches to ensure every cup is a perfect expression of its origin.

Whether you're looking for a quiet corner to focus, a vibrant space to meet friends, or simply your morning fuel, you're always welcome at Rio Java.
